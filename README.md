# TURAVOZ 🇵🇰

## Pakistan's Online Marketplace & Training Platform

TURAVOZ is a modern Pakistani online marketplace and training platform designed to connect customers, sellers, shops and learners in one place.

### 🛍️ Marketplace
- Browse products
- Product details
- Shopping cart
- Orders
- Seller shops
- Seller dashboard

### 👤 Account
- Login
- Customer account
- Order management
- Contact & support

### 🏪 For Sellers
- Seller dashboard
- Add and manage products
- Manage orders
- Seller community
- Grow your online business

### 📚 Training
TURAVOZ aims to provide useful digital and business training to help people learn new skills and grow their online businesses.

### 🇵🇰 Made for Pakistan
TURAVOZ is designed with Pakistani customers and sellers in mind.
